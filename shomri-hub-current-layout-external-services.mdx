## Services

Shomri Hub relies on the following external services as part of its core functionality:

- **Clerk** – authentication and user management.
- **Convex** – backend data storage and server-side logic.
- **Gleap** – in-product feedback and bug reporting.
- **iTunes Search API (proxied)** – app metadata and search results for the App Store experience.
- **PostHog** – product analytics and usage insights.
- **HyperDX** – logging, traces, and observability.

<Callout kind="info">

These services are implementation dependencies of Shomri Hub. You do not configure or manage these integrations directly from your Shomri Hub workspace.

</Callout>

## Why they matter

These external services handle critical parts of the Shomri Hub experience, such as secure sign-in, persistent data, app catalog information, analytics, and operational monitoring.

Understanding that Shomri Hub depends on these providers helps you evaluate reliability, data flows, and compliance requirements in your own environment and procurement process.
