## Purpose of the Support tab

The Support tab in the Shomri App bottom navigation gives users a single place to reach your support team, with a primary in-app chat plus additional channels.

Each option appears as a card that either opens an embedded chat experience, launches WhatsApp, or triggers an operating system deep link (such as opening the dialer or mail client) when those channels are available.

<Callout kind="info">

Current Support options include Chat with Us and WhatsApp. Call, Text, and Email are planned channels; you can surface them as disabled or hide them from the UI until they are ready.

</Callout>

## Behavior and flow

When a user taps or clicks a Support card:

- **Chat with Us** opens the in-app support chat or embedded widget, anchored in the current Shomri App session.
- **WhatsApp** uses a URL such as `https://wa.me/` or a platform-specific scheme to open the WhatsApp app with your support number.
- **Call support** uses a `tel:` link to open the dialer with the number prefilled once enabled.
- **Text support** uses an `sms:` link to open the messaging app with your support number.
- **Email support** relies on a `mailto:` link so the OS opens the preferred email client with your support inbox.

Successful configuration routes the user directly into the correct support channel without extra intermediate screens or confirmation dialogs.

## Configuration guidelines

Configure each Support option by setting the correct target for its card `href` and ensuring the corresponding integration is active in your environment.

<Callout kind="info">

Use staging endpoints while developing and replace them with production values before launch. Test active channels (Chat and WhatsApp) on all target platforms (web, iOS, Android) to confirm that links or widgets open correctly.

</Callout>

### Chat with Us integration

Chat with Us represents your primary in-app support experience.

- Use the card `href` or click handler to trigger your chat widget or open a dedicated Support chat screen.
- Initialize any third-party chat SDKs or internal messaging services before rendering the Support tab.
- Decide whether to always open a new conversation or resume the most recent thread for the current user.

### WhatsApp deep link

WhatsApp supports platform-independent deep links using HTTPS URLs.

Common patterns:

- `https://wa.me/` followed by your full phone number in international format.
- Optional query parameters to prefill the message text with context such as the app version or user identifier.

If you target native apps, you can also use platform schemes where supported, but start with HTTPS links for broad compatibility.

### Call, Text, and Email coming soon

Prepare deep links for future channels even if the UI keeps them hidden or disabled.

- **Call support**: set `href` to `tel:+10000000000` with your international-format support line.
- **Text support**: use `sms:+10000000000` or a platform-equivalent scheme to open the default messaging app.
- **Email support**: use a `mailto:` URL such as `mailto:support@your-company.com` with optional subject and body query parameters.

Align your UI state (hidden, disabled, or tagged as coming soon) with the real availability of each channel so users know which options they can rely on today.

## Placeholder values and production setup

Because Support contact details are specific to each deployment, Shomri does not hard-code URLs or phone numbers.

Use clear, non-production placeholders during development, such as:

- `mailto:support+staging@your-company.com`
- `tel:+19999999999`
- `sms:+19999999999`
- `https://wa.me/19999999999?text=Support%20staging`
- A staging endpoint or sandbox configuration for your in-app chat service

Before releasing to users:

- Replace all placeholders with your live Support endpoints.
- Verify that your support team monitors every active channel.
- Align the labels and descriptive text on the cards with your support policies (for example, “Live chat (business hours)” vs “WhatsApp within 24 hours”).
